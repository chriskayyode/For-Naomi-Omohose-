To my Naomi, my Omohose.

Happy birthday, Ajayi Naomi Omohose! ❤️🎂

I honestly don't even know where to start because how do you put someone who means so much to you into words and somehow make it enough?

Naomi, one thing I know for sure is that having you in my life is something I'll never take for granted. You are genuinely one of the sweetest, most caring, thoughtful, and supportive people I know. You're the kind of person who checks up on me, asks how my day went, remembers when I'm writing exams, texts to wish me well, and makes sure I know that someone is rooting for me. You show up in ways that might seem little to you, but they mean so much more than you probably realise.

And I hope you know that I notice. I notice the calls, the messages, the encouragement, the thoughtfulness, and the way you consistently make an effort to be present in my life. Even when I'm caught up in my own world, you still find ways to remind me that I'm not alone. That kind of friendship is rare, Naomi, and I hope life gives you back every bit of the goodness you keep pouring into other people.

You're such a beautiful person, inside and out, and I genuinely wouldn't trade you for anything. You have this way of making people feel cared for, supported, and remembered, and I'm so glad I get to experience that side of you. If there's one thing I want you to carry into this new year of your life, it's the knowledge that you matter, that you're appreciated, and that you deserve to be loved just as intentionally as you love other people.

And then there's the lawyer you're becoming! 🥹❤️

Naomi, I'm incredibly proud of you. Not just because you're a law student, but because of the person you are while pursuing everything you want to become. Watching you take on your responsibilities, seeing the work you do as Editor-in-Chief at Fulli Law, and knowing that you're building towards something meaningful makes me genuinely happy for you. You have so much potential, and I believe in you and your dreams more than I can adequately explain.

I know the journey won't always be easy. There will be demanding days, difficult seasons, moments of uncertainty, and times when things don't happen as quickly as you'd hoped. But I hope you never lose sight of the woman you're becoming. I hope you keep trusting the process, keep putting in the work, and keep giving yourself the same patience and understanding you so freely give to others. I truly believe that one day, you'll look back at everything you're doing now and understand why it was worth it. And when that day comes, I hope you remember that I was rooting for you all along.

As you step into this new chapter, my prayer is that God goes ahead of you and makes every crooked path straight. May He grant you the desires of your heart, bless the work of your hands, and bring to fruition every good thing you've been hoping, praying, and working for. May your expectations not be cut short. May the doors you've been knocking on open, may the opportunities meant for you find you, and may you never lack the wisdom, strength, favour, or courage to become everything you've dreamed of being.

I pray this year brings you genuine happiness, good health, peace of mind, beautiful friendships, unexpected blessings, and so many reasons to smile. May you receive good news that makes you cry happy tears. May your efforts yield results. May you find yourself in rooms you once only prayed to enter. And above all, may you always have the reassurance that even when life gets overwhelming, God is still working things out for your good.

And Naomi, there's something I also wanted to say. I had plans to get you something for your birthday, something I genuinely wanted you to have, but unfortunately, those plans fell through. I might not be able to do what I had in mind right now, but please never mistake that for you deserving any less. You deserve the very best, and I truly mean that. I hope I'll get the opportunity to make it happen because you deserve to be celebrated in ways that go beyond words.

Thank you for being you, Naomi. Thank you for being such a wonderful friend, for checking on me, for believing in me, for remembering my important moments, and for being one of those people who make life a little easier simply by being part of it. I might not always express it as much as I should, but I'm genuinely grateful for you, and I hope you never have to question how much you mean to me.

Happy birthday once again, my Omohose. ❤️🥹

Here's to a new year of becoming, winning, growing, healing, and receiving all the beautiful things you've spent so much time giving to others. Here's to the lawyer you're becoming, the woman you're growing into, and the many wonderful things that are still ahead of you.

The world has so much waiting for you, Naomi, and I genuinely cannot wait to see you have your moment.

I love and appreciate you, and I'm always rooting for you. May this be one of your most beautiful years yet. ❤️🫂

Happy birthday, Naomi! 🎂❤️
